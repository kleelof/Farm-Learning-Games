Do you know how to do math?Do you know how to catch a duck?If you answerd YES and YES, then you are ready 
to play Digit Ducks!When the game begins, you will be given a math 
problem. One digit will be missing from the 
problem or answer. Catch the duck with the 
missing digit and drop it on the big red '?'.Solve as many problems as you can before the 
duck pen gets full!
