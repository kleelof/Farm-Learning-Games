Gary Goat is hungry, and you and farmer Fred need to feed him. But,unlike other goats, Gary can't eat just anything. When the game begins, choose the type of words you would like gary toeat. Once you have chosen the word type, farmer Fred will begin throwingwords.Use your mouse to guide gary to the correct type of words. Once a wordhe likes to eat is on his head, press your mouse button to eat the word.If gary eats too many of the wrong words, he will get tooill to eat any more.
